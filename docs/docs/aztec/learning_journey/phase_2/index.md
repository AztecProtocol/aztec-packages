# Phase 2: Privacy-First Thinking

*Target Audience: Developers familiar with traditional blockchain*  
*Learning Approach: Mental model shifting, comparative analysis*

---

Now that you understand **why** privacy matters, it's time to learn **how** to think about privacy-preserving systems. This phase will shift your mental models from traditional blockchain thinking to privacy-first approaches.

## What You'll Learn

By the end of Phase 2, you'll understand:

✅ **How privacy-first systems differ conceptually** from transparent blockchains  
✅ **The mental shift from accounts to UTXOs** and why it's necessary  
✅ **Hybrid execution models** and when to use private vs public functions  
✅ **State management patterns** unique to privacy-preserving systems  

## The Mental Model Challenge

If you're coming from Ethereum or other transparent blockchains, you'll need to **unlearn** some assumptions and **relearn** new patterns. This isn't just a technical shift - it's a fundamental change in how you think about blockchain applications.
