# Aztec's Mission

## Privacy-First Infrastructure

Aztec Protocol is building **privacy-first blockchain infrastructure** that doesn't force users to choose between trustlessness and privacy.

**Our Vision:** A blockchain where privacy is the default, transparency is optional, and users control their information disclosure.

This means:
- **Private by default:** Your transactions and balances are encrypted and hidden
- **Programmably transparent:** You can choose to reveal specific information when needed
- **Fully decentralized:** No trusted third parties or centralized operators
- **Ethereum compatible:** Seamless integration with the existing DeFi ecosystem

## Technical Innovation for Human Values

Aztec combines cutting-edge cryptography with practical usability:

### Zero-Knowledge Proofs
Prove something is true without revealing the underlying information. For example, prove you have enough funds for a transaction without revealing your balance.

### Note-based Privacy
Private state management that protects your information while enabling complex smart contract interactions.

### Hybrid Execution
Choose private or public execution based on your needs - the same contract can handle both.

### L2 Scalability
Fast, low-cost transactions without sacrificing security or decentralization.

But technology is just the means. **The end goal is human flourishing in a digital world.**

## Building the Future We Want

Aztec isn't just about better technology - it's about better outcomes:

### For Individuals
- **Financial sovereignty without surveillance** - Control your money without constant monitoring
- **Freedom to experiment and learn privately** - Try new investments and strategies without public exposure
- **Protection from discrimination and exploitation** - Prevent others from using your financial information against you
- **Control over personal information disclosure** - Choose what to reveal and when

### For Businesses
- **Competitive advantages through strategy protection** - Build and test new products without competitors seeing every move
- **Fair negotiations without information asymmetries** - Negotiate deals without revealing all your assets
- **Private operations with selective transparency** - Keep internal operations private while proving compliance
- **Innovation without fear of immediate copying** - Experiment with new business models safely

### For Society
- **Democratic processes free from coercion** - Vote and participate without fear of retaliation
- **Economic systems that don't exploit transparency** - Prevent front-running, MEV, and other extraction mechanisms
- **Innovation environments that reward risk-taking** - Create space for genuine innovation and experimentation
- **Financial systems that respect human dignity** - Build technology that serves people, not surveils them

## How Aztec Works (High Level)

Aztec enables privacy through a sophisticated architecture:

1. **Private Execution:** Smart contract functions can run privately on your device, generating proofs of correct execution
2. **Public Verification:** These proofs are verified publicly without revealing private information
3. **Hybrid State:** Contracts can manage both private and public state, giving developers flexibility
4. **Ethereum Settlement:** All transactions ultimately settle on Ethereum for security and finality

This architecture means you get:
- The privacy of client-side execution
- The security of public verification
- The composability of public blockchains
- The finality of Ethereum settlement

## The Path Forward

We're building technology that:
- **Protects privacy** while enabling compliance
- **Prevents exploitation** while allowing innovation  
- **Respects dignity** while maintaining security
- **Empowers individuals** while supporting legitimate oversight

This isn't about avoiding rules - it's about building better systems that protect human rights while enabling new forms of cooperation and value creation.

## Key Takeaway

Aztec's mission is to build blockchain infrastructure that serves human values. By making privacy the default and transparency optional, we create space for innovation, protect vulnerable populations, and enable more fair participation in the digital economy.
