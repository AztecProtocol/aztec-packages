# Phase 1: Why Privacy Matters

*Target Audience: Anyone interested in blockchain privacy*  
*Learning Approach: Narrative-driven, motivation-focused*

---

Welcome to your journey into privacy-preserving blockchain technology. Before diving into technical concepts, it's crucial to understand **why** privacy matters in blockchain contexts and what problems we're solving.

## What You'll Learn

By the end of Phase 1, you'll understand:

✅ **Why blockchain transparency creates real problems** for individuals and businesses  
✅ **How privacy differs from secrecy** and why this distinction matters  
✅ **Aztec's mission** to build privacy-first blockchain infrastructure  
✅ **The motivation** to learn about privacy-preserving technology
